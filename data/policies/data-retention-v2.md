# Data Retention Policy

This policy defines how long Northbeam Cloud keeps customer data, backups and logs, and how that data
is deleted at the end of its retention period. It applies to every system that stores customer or
company information.

## Principles

Data is kept only as long as it is needed to provide the service, to meet a legal obligation or to
investigate a security event. Each system owner documents what data the system stores and which
retention rule in this policy applies to it.

## Customer data

Customer data is deleted within 90 days after contract end. During that period the customer can ask
for an export of its data. Deletion is performed by the platform team on request from the account
manager once the contract is closed.

## Backups

Backups are kept for 90 days. Older backups are removed by the backup lifecycle rules. Backups are
not used for any purpose other than restoring the service.

## Logs

Security logs are kept for 1 year to support investigations and audits. Application debug logs are
kept for a shorter period set by the platform team.

## Legal holds

If a legal hold applies, the Head of Security can suspend deletion for the data concerned. Every hold
is recorded with its reason and is lifted as soon as it is no longer needed.
