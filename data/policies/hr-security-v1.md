# Human Resources Security Policy

This policy describes the security steps that Northbeam Cloud takes before, during and after
employment. It applies to employees and, where stated, to contractors.

## Screening

Background checks are performed for all employees where legally permitted. The checks are carried
out by a specialised provider before the start date and follow the law of the country where the
employee is hired. Contractors with access to customer data are screened by their agency to an
equivalent standard.

## Agreements

Confidentiality agreements are signed at hire, before any access to company systems is granted. The
employment contract also states the duty to follow company security policies and to report security
incidents.

## During employment

Managers make sure that their team members complete the required security training and that access
changes when someone changes role. Security responsibilities are part of every job description for
engineering and support roles.

## Disciplinary process

Breaches of security policies are handled through the normal disciplinary process, which is applied
fairly and takes into account the seriousness of the breach and whether it was intentional.

## End of employment

The People team informs IT of every departure in advance so that access can be removed on time and
company equipment can be collected. Confidentiality obligations continue after employment ends.
