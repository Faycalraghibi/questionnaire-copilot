# Information Security Policy

Northbeam Cloud provides a logistics planning platform to business customers. This policy sets the
overall direction for protecting customer information and the systems that process it. It applies to
all employees, contractors and systems operated by Northbeam Cloud.

## Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish a basic security program for a growing company. Specific
controls are described in supporting standards, which are added as the company matures.

## Governance

The security program is owned by the Head of Security, who reports to the CEO on security matters
when needed. Security policies are reviewed every 2 years, or earlier if a significant change in the
business or in regulation requires it. Changes are approved by the Head of Security and shared with
all staff by email.

## Training

Every employee completes security awareness training at hire as part of onboarding. The training
covers data handling, password practices and how to report a suspected incident. There is no
phishing simulation program at this time; the security team will consider one when the company grows.
Completion of onboarding training is recorded by the People team.

## Exceptions

Exceptions to any security policy must be requested in writing with a business justification and
must be approved by the Head of Security. Approved exceptions are recorded and reviewed periodically.

## Enforcement

Violations of this policy may lead to disciplinary action. Employees are encouraged to report
security concerns to the security team without fear of retaliation.
