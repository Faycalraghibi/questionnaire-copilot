# Information Security Policy

Northbeam Cloud provides a logistics planning platform to business customers. This policy sets the
overall direction for protecting the information that customers entrust to us and the systems that
process it. It applies to all employees, contractors and systems operated by Northbeam Cloud.

## Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to make security a routine part of how Northbeam Cloud builds and runs
its service. Supporting standards and procedures describe the specific controls. This policy explains
who is responsible for them and how they stay current.

## Governance

The security program is owned by the Head of Security and reviewed by the CEO annually. The Head of
Security reports on risks, incidents and progress against the security roadmap to the leadership
team. All security policies are reviewed at least once a year, and every change is approved by the
Head of Security before it is published on the internal wiki. Each policy records its version and
effective date so that staff always know which text applies.

## Training

Every employee and contractor completes security awareness training at hire and every year after.
The training covers data handling, authentication hygiene, recognising social engineering and how
to report a suspected incident. Phishing simulations run every quarter, and anyone who clicks a
simulated link receives a short follow-up module. The People team tracks completion and reminds
managers when a member of their team is overdue.

## Exceptions

Exceptions to any security policy must be requested in writing with a business justification and an
end date, and must be approved by the Head of Security. Approved exceptions are recorded in a
register and reviewed when they expire.

## Enforcement

Violations of this policy may lead to disciplinary action. Employees are encouraged to report
security concerns to the security team without fear of retaliation.
