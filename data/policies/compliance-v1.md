# Compliance Policy

This policy summarises the external assurance that Northbeam Cloud provides to customers and the
commitments it makes about where customer data is stored. It is maintained by the Head of Security
and updated whenever an audit or a commitment changes.

## Purpose

Customers and their auditors rely on independent evidence that Northbeam Cloud operates its controls
as described. This policy states which evidence exists and how customers can obtain it.

## Certifications

A SOC 2 Type II report is available under NDA, covering the period ending 2026-06-30. Customers can
request it through their account manager. Northbeam Cloud is not ISO 27001 certified; certification
is planned for 2027, and the gap assessment for it is under way.

## Data location

Customer data is stored and processed in the EU only. Support staff outside the EU do not have access
to customer data. Any future change to this commitment would be announced to customers in advance.

## Customer audits

Customers can send security questionnaires to their account manager. Answers are prepared by the
security team from the current versions of company policies and are reviewed before they are sent.

## Regulatory requirements

The legal and security teams track the data protection laws that apply to Northbeam Cloud and its
customers, and update policies when those laws change.
