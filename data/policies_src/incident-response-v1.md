# Incident Response Policy

This policy describes how Northbeam Cloud detects, handles and learns from security incidents. It
applies to every employee and contractor and to every system that stores or processes customer data.

## Reporting

Anyone who suspects a security incident reports it immediately in the security channel or through
the security mailbox. Reports can be made without fear of blame. The person on call acknowledges the
report and opens an incident record.

## Severity and roles

Each incident is given a severity from 1 to 4 based on its impact on customers and data. For severity
1 and 2 incidents, an incident lead coordinates the response, a scribe keeps the timeline and a
communications owner prepares updates for customers and leadership. The Head of Security is
informed of every severity 1 and 2 incident.

## On-call

A security on-call rotation covers 24/7. The rotation is staffed by trained engineers from the
security and platform teams, and the schedule is published in the paging tool so that alerts always
reach a named person.

## Notification

Affected customers are notified within 72 hours of a confirmed breach. The notice describes what
happened, which data was involved, what Northbeam Cloud has done and what the customer may need to
do. Regulators are notified where the law requires it.

## Testing

The incident response plan is tested once a year with a tabletop exercise. Lessons from the exercise
and from real incidents are recorded and turned into tracked improvements.

## Post-incident review

Every severity 1 and 2 incident ends with a written review within ten working days. The review
focuses on causes and fixes rather than individual blame.
