# Vendor Management Policy

This policy describes how Northbeam Cloud selects, contracts and monitors the vendors that support
its service, in particular vendors that process customer data on its behalf.

## Classification

Every vendor is recorded in the vendor register with an owner and a classification. A vendor is
critical when it processes customer data or when its failure would stop the service. Other vendors
are classified as standard.

## Assessment

Critical vendors are assessed every year. The assessment reviews the vendor's security reports and
certifications, its incident history and any changes to the service it provides. Standard vendors are
reviewed when their contract is renewed. Findings are tracked until they are resolved or accepted by
the Head of Security.

## Subprocessors

The subprocessor list is published on the website, and customers are notified 30 days before changes
so that they can raise concerns. The list names each subprocessor, its purpose and the location where
it processes data.

## Contracts

A data processing agreement is signed with every subprocessor before it receives customer data.
Contracts with critical vendors also include security obligations, breach notification duties and
the right to terminate if those obligations are not met.

## Offboarding vendors

When a vendor relationship ends, the owner confirms that access has been removed and that customer
data held by the vendor has been returned or deleted.
